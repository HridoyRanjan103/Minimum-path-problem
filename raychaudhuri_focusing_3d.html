What is physically modeled

The compact-mass view uses a softened point potential only to keep the numerical integration finite. Its Ricci curvature is therefore a small core artifact; outside the core, focusing comes from Weyl tidal shear. The cloud has positive density and hence positive Rkk, which directly focuses null bundles.
